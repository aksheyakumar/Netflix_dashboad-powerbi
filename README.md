# Netflix_dashboad-powerbi

**1. Netflix Dashboard : Netflix Insights Dashboard**
  This is an interactive dashboard where we show different types of charts from these charts we can see what type of content is popular or most watched around the world.

**2. Description :**
   The Netflix Analytics dashbord is a visually engaging and analytically design power bi dashboard to find out what types of content is being consumed by the people around the world and this also help content makers to find which type of content they should make so, people found it engaging like from the dataset we found-out that around 70% of content is being circulated on netflix are movies and only 30% are tv shows this show that many people like to watch movies rather than tv shows from this we can also conclude a point the tv shows are long and have many season and most of the people don't get enough time to watch a tv show and also there are season which makes it more changelling for a person to watch tv show and they found movies as their watchlist.we can also see that after 2020 more content is circulated on the netflix prior to 2020 there is not much content is circulated on netflix from this we can found out that netflix increase it's popularity around the world which means many new content and content makers see netflix as a way to describe their stories.
